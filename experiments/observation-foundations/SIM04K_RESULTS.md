# SIM-04K — Action / Conservation / Backreaction Results

**Status:** synthetic energetic-consistency benchmark complete  
**Claim level:** methodology only; not evidence for a physical SoCT memory field.

## Central question

> If `M` is interpreted as a physical field rather than merely a hidden predictive state, do the same parameters that fit its trajectory also predict an independent energy/backreaction observable?

SIM-04K uses the causal completion introduced in SIM-04J:

```math
\partial_t^2M
+\gamma\partial_tM
-c_M^2\nabla^2M
+\omega_M^2M
=gC_{obs}.
```

The late-time mapping is

```math
c_M^2=\gamma D_M,
\qquad
\omega_M^2=\gamma\beta.
```

For periodic boundaries the field obeys

```math
\frac{dE_M}{dt}
=g\int C_{obs}\,\partial_tM\,dx
-\gamma\int(\partial_tM)^2dx.
```

The second term defines the independently observed damping/bath heat channel.

## Design

A periodic 16-site one-dimensional field is driven by four source histories.

```text
A, B = parameter-fitting histories
C, D = held-out histories
```

The planted parameters are

```text
gamma  = 5.0
beta   = 0.08
D_M    = 0.18
lambda = 0.70
```

with

```math
c_M=\sqrt{\gamma D_M}\approx0.94868,
```

and

```math
\omega_M=\sqrt{\gamma\beta}\approx0.63246.
```

Probe observations are noisy linear readouts of `lambda M` at selected sites/times.

The fitting rule is intentionally strict:

1. fit `(beta, D_M, lambda)` **only from probe trajectories on A/B**;
2. freeze the fitted field parameters;
3. score probe prediction on held-out C/D;
4. predict damping heat on C/D with **no additional heat/backreaction amplitude fit**.

Probe noise is

```text
sigma_probe = 0.0015
```

and heat-channel noise is

```text
sigma_heat = 0.0020.
```

Fifty deterministic noise realizations are evaluated.

## Synthetic generators

Three generators use the same field-like probe trajectory.

### 1. `action_consistent`

Probe trajectory and damping heat both come from the same causal field.

### 2. `probe_only`

The probe trajectory is identical in law, but the independent heat channel contains no excess field-associated signal.

This is a deliberate falsifier for the interpretation of the probe residual as the physical field defined by the action scaffold.

### 3. `source_heat`

The probe trajectory again follows the causal field, but the heat channel is generated by a simpler instantaneous source-power law proportional to

```math
\int C_{obs}^2 dx
```

rather than by

```math
\gamma\int(\partial_tM)^2dx.
```

The source-power null is allowed one scale fitted on A/B and frozen before C/D.

## Results

Mean values across 50 noise realizations:

| Generator | fitted `beta` | fitted `D_M` | fitted `lambda` | held-out probe RMSE | action-linked heat RMSE | zero-heat RMSE | source-power heat RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **action consistent** | 0.0808 | 0.1780 | 0.7001 | **0.00149** | **0.00194** | 0.08630 | 0.01404 |
| probe only / no heat | 0.0808 | 0.1780 | 0.7001 | **0.00149** | 0.08643 | **0.00193** | 0.00198 |
| source-power heat | 0.0808 | 0.1780 | 0.7001 | **0.00149** | 0.03906 | 0.04877 | **0.00198** |

The action-linked heat prediction is at the imposed heat-noise scale only when the data generator actually obeys the action-linked energy ledger.

## Interpretation

### 1. Probe fit alone is not enough

All three generators produce essentially identical field-like probe behavior.

Accordingly, fitting only the probe histories recovers approximately

```text
beta   ~ 0.0808
D_M    ~ 0.1780
lambda ~ 0.7001
```

and predicts the held-out probe data at the noise floor in **all three cases**.

Thus

```text
excellent probe prediction != evidence for the physical-field interpretation.
```

The independent energy channel is what distinguishes the mechanisms in this benchmark.

### 2. The action-consistent generator passes a parameter-transfer test

For `action_consistent` data, parameters fitted only to the probe trajectory predict held-out damping heat with

```text
RMSE ~ 0.00194
```

against

```text
sigma_heat = 0.0020.
```

No heat-scale parameter was fitted.

The zero-heat null gives

```text
RMSE ~ 0.08630,
```

and the independently scaled source-power null gives

```text
RMSE ~ 0.01404.
```

So the linked field trajectory and energy-exchange law contain predictive information beyond the probe trajectory alone in this synthetic setting.

### 3. The same constraint can falsify the field interpretation

For the `probe_only` generator, the probe trajectory still fits perfectly well, but the action-linked model predicts a heat signal that is not present:

```text
action heat RMSE ~ 0.08643.
```

The correct zero-heat null remains at

```text
~0.00193.
```

Therefore the energetic constraint is not merely another way for H2 to win. It can reject the candidate physical completion while leaving a phenomenological history-dependent probe model viable.

### 4. A different physical heat mechanism is also recoverable

For `source_heat` data, the action-linked heat model gives

```text
RMSE ~ 0.03906,
```

while the source-power heat null, whose scale is fitted only on A/B, reaches

```text
RMSE ~ 0.00198.
```

Again, the independent channel selects the mechanism that actually generated the data rather than automatically favoring the memory-field action.



This is an implementation consistency check, not physical evidence.

## What SIM-04K establishes

Only the following narrow methodology statements are supported:

1. the causal memory completion admits a linked flat-background energy ledger;
2. parameters fitted to field/probe trajectories can make a no-retuning prediction for damping/backreaction heat;
3. this prediction succeeds on held-out synthetic data generated by the same action/exchange structure;
4. the prediction fails when the same probe trajectory is deliberately generated without the required heat channel;
5. a different source-linked heat law wins when that alternative generated the independent channel.
