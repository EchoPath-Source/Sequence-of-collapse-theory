# Node–Source–Origin (NSO): Recursive Origin Graduation v0.1

Status: proposed research construct, deferred implementation. Source: November 2026 design discussion. Original user mechanics below; engineering alternatives explicitly marked. No claim of physical validation, consciousness, novelty or product readiness.

## Original construct
Discrete temporal rotation resolves instantaneous self-reference: active Source S_t completes a finite working-memory sweep through compression C_t; upon a coherence-gated selection event, S_t freezes into historical Origin O_t and C_t elevates into S_(t+1), biased by O_t. Distinguish vertical abstraction (D1 events → D2 motifs → D3 context → Omega Origin) from temporal generations. Graduation may begin at first compression, not only terminal sweep. Too-early capture risks destructive out-of-phase consolidation; too-late capture risks decay. Proposed peak gate R(t)>=theta_c and dR/dt approximately zero, followed by selection gain amplifying signal and discarding noise. Exact gain, thresholds and timescale remain open.

Origins communicate across scales: old slow invariants, medium contextual schemas, new fast active priors. Top-down conditioning supplies structural bias; bottom-up resonance validates or challenges older priors. Attenuation/gating prevents dogmatism. Frozen payload differs from mutable confidence, relevance and routing authority. Age is not proof of truth.

Original fitness W_i = mean_R_i^alpha * ln(1+N_i) * exp(-lambda*delta_t_i), where mean_R is average peak coherence of successful spawned Sources (0..1), N successful downstream Origins, delta_t cycles since last success, lambda nonnegative decay. Alpha>=1. Original inverted annealing alpha(g)=alpha_min+(alpha_max-alpha_min)*exp(-k*g), k>=0; early precision, later exploration. Log damping gives diminishing returns, not a guaranteed monopoly cap. Coherence and prolificacy do not prove generalization.

High score grants hub influence; low score after grace period prunes links and archives or garbage-collects state; N=0 during grace receives W_init for k_grace cycles. Preferential attachment plus pruning may yield a hub-and-spoke backbone, a hypothesis not a guaranteed topology. After pruning: (1) renormalize remaining influence, (2) merge redundant Origins, combine counts and coherence, (3) re-anchor orphaned Sources to compatible high-scoring parent/sibling or abort/reset, (4) audit baseline drift against foundational invariants and lower alpha only if stable. Repeat expansion and consolidation.

## Proposed engineering safeguards (not original equations)
Keep existing V10 readiness gates; peak derivative alone also detects minima/plateaus; require meaningful activity, sustained readiness and useful information. Use logical promotion first, not tensor resizing. Distinguish immutable payload from mutable metadata. State machine: active node → compression candidate → graduated Source → Origin candidate → probationary → established → hub/dormant → merged/archived; allow context-dependent reactivation. Preserve lineage, unique IDs and generation order; no instantaneous cyclic evaluation.
Normalize p_i=W_i/sum(W); define empty/all-zero fallback (no actuation or explicit protected uniform allocation). Optional exploration p_i=(1-epsilon)*W_i/sum(W)+epsilon/|A|; test caps and opportunity-normalized yield. Normalization conserves allocation, not vector bias; bound actual actuation norm. Optional utility Q_i^beta and pseudocount n0: W_i=Q_i^beta*mean_R_i^alpha*ln(1+N_i+n0)*exp(-lambda*delta_t_i). Compare original, starter weight, pseudocount and utility arms separately. Separate protected invariant retention from routing weight; independently test invariants, not age or agreement alone.
Candidate coupling B_ij=p_i*G_ij*exp(-mu*abs(g_j-g_i)); contextual compatibility G bounded, attenuation mu>=0. Use previous committed snapshot for feedback; bounded influence and controlled transition cadence. Bottom-up updates metadata, never silently rewrites payload.
Merge only when memory, phase (circular), behavioral and contextual similarity pass and held-out equivalence holds. Candidate S_ij=sum(w_x*S_x), weights nonnegative sum one. Deduplicate descendant IDs; for disjoint histories mean_R_merge=(N_i*R_i+N_j*R_j)/(N_i+N_j); handle zero denominator explicitly. Preserve original IDs via alias/provenance; no irreversible deletion before validation.
Fractional credit N_eff_i=sum_b c_ib, c>=0 and sum_i c_ib=1 per successful branch. Communication share is a proxy, not causal attribution; test removal/substitution and opportunity-adjusted success. Avoid double counting shared descendants.
Consolidation transaction: snapshot → propose prune/merge → resolve orphan references → normalize → validate constraints and behavior → atomic commit or rollback. Active Sources pin snapshot until safe boundary; abort/restart if invalid. Preserve archive and audit trail; bounded active/archive budgets. Homeostasis counter h advances on stable useful audit, holds on uncertainty, decrements to max(0,h-1) on instability; substitute h for g in annealing. Compare fixed, scheduled, reversible schedules; use independent task utility and contradiction evidence, not only trunk agreement.
