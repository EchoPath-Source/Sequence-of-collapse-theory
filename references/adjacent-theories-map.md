# Adjacent Theories Map — Sequence of Collapse Theory

**Status:** Literature-positioning and claim-boundary reference  
**Purpose:** Map Sequence of Collapse Theory (SoCT) and Planck Nucleation Theory (PNT) to nearby research programs for comparison, discoverability, falsification design, and academically safe framing.

## Purpose

SoCT is positioned as a speculative but falsifiable unification framework adjacent to objective-collapse theories, Diósi-Penrose gravitational collapse proposals, Penrose-Hameroff Orch OR, ER=EPR, black-hole information preservation, gravitational memory effects, holography, emergent spacetime, and cosmic-web dark-sector phenomenology. These fields do not validate SoCT, but they define the research neighborhood in which SoCT's hypotheses can be compared, constrained, or falsified.

This map is a literature bridge, not an evidence ledger. Its purpose is to make the repo academically legible when readers approach from neighboring topics while preserving the distinction between precedent, analogy, and empirical support.

## Boundary

- Adjacent does not mean confirmed.
- Conceptual overlap does not mean physical equivalence.
- Literature precedent does not prove SoCT or PNT.
- Discoverability is not evidence and should not become keyword stuffing.
- SoCT remains speculative and falsifiable.
- Every adjacency below includes both a difference and an explicit claim boundary.

A safe recurring formulation is:

> This adjacent theory defines part of the research neighborhood in which SoCT can be compared, constrained, or falsified.

## Core Adjacencies

### 1. Diósi-Penrose Objective Reduction

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Diósi and Penrose independently developed gravity-related approaches in which sufficiently distinct mass distributions can become unstable as quantum superpositions, motivating objective state reduction tied to gravity rather than to a conscious observer.

**SoCT overlap.** Both connect collapse physics and gravity.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT extends the research question into a layered sequence that includes a memory-field channel, an observer-state channel, and possible gravitational/cosmological consequences. Those additional components are not supplied by the Diósi-Penrose program.

**Claim boundary.** Diósi-Penrose objective reduction does not validate SoCT. It provides an adjacent gravity-collapse research lane against which SoCT's collapse assumptions should be compared.

### 2. Penrose-Hameroff Orch OR

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Orch OR proposes that biologically orchestrated quantum processes in neuronal microtubules terminate through objective reduction and are relevant to conscious experience. The proposal remains debated in consciousness science and quantum biology.

**SoCT overlap.** Both place consciousness-adjacent questions near collapse physics.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT does not depend on microtubules and should not inherit Orch OR's biological mechanism. In the current SoCT scaffold, observer-state or conscious-access variables are hypotheses to be operationalized and tested as separate channels.

**Claim boundary.** Orch OR does not prove SoCT, consciousness-driven collapse, or a universal consciousness field. Its value here is comparative: it shows one established speculative program that explicitly connects objective reduction with consciousness.

### 3. ER=EPR

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Maldacena and Susskind proposed that Einstein-Rosen bridges and Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen entanglement may be deeply related, initially in the context of entangled black holes and later as a broader conjectural connection between entanglement and geometry.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT/PNT likewise explores whether information, entanglement residue, gravity, and geometry may be linked.

**SoCT difference.** ER=EPR centers on an entanglement/geometry correspondence. SoCT centers on collapse history and memory accumulation as candidate physical structure.

**Claim boundary.** ER=EPR does not prove SoCT/PNT. It is an adjacent framework suggesting that quantum information and spacetime geometry may not be separable at a fundamental level.

### 4. GRW / CSL Objective Collapse

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** GRW and continuous-spontaneous-localization (CSL) models modify standard quantum dynamics so that state reduction is an objective physical process, typically stochastic and negligible microscopically while becoming effective macroscopically.

**SoCT overlap.** Both treat collapse as potentially physical rather than only an update of an observer's knowledge.

**SoCT difference.** GRW/CSL supply specific stochastic dynamical structures. SoCT adds memory-field, observer-state, gravitational, and cosmological interpretation layers that require independent mathematical definition.

**Claim boundary.** Objective-collapse literature supports the legitimacy of constructing experimentally testable collapse models. It does not support SoCT specifically unless SoCT produces distinct quantitative predictions and survives comparison with those models.

### 5. Decoherence / Quantum Darwinism

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Environment-induced decoherence suppresses interference between selected states, while quantum Darwinism studies how redundant records of selected states proliferate into the environment and support effectively objective classical information.

**SoCT overlap.** Both address the transition from quantum possibility to stable experienced/classical structure.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT treats environmental interaction as a baseline or first narrowing and separately hypothesizes memory and observer-state channels. Those extra channels cannot be inferred from decoherence alone.

**Claim boundary.** Decoherence and quantum Darwinism do not prove SoCT. They are mandatory comparison baselines for any SoCT claim about collapse, classicality, information access, or observer dependence.

### 6. Black-Hole Information Paradox

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Black-hole evaporation raises a fundamental quantum-gravity problem concerning unitarity, horizons, entropy, and how information is encoded or recovered.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT/PNT treats black holes as candidate high-compression information environments and, in P6, explores them as possible memory-compression nodes.

**SoCT difference.** Mainstream work studies mechanisms such as horizon encoding, radiation correlations, holography, entanglement structure, and quantum-gravity effects. PNT adds speculative memory-compression and parent-child transfer ideas not established by that literature.

**Claim boundary.** The black-hole information problem motivates comparison but does not prove memory transmission, child universes, PNT, or a collapse-memory substrate.

### 7. Holography / Entropy-Area Ideas

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Black-hole thermodynamics links entropy with horizon area, while holographic approaches relate gravitational bulk information to lower-dimensional boundary descriptions. Holographic entanglement entropy further relates boundary entanglement measures to bulk geometric areas in AdS/CFT settings.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT/PNT also treats information and gravitational structure as potentially linked.

**SoCT difference.** Holography is fundamentally a boundary/information and duality framework. SoCT emphasizes accumulated collapse history, memory variables, and sequence dynamics.

**Claim boundary.** Holographic and entropy-area relations are adjacent to SoCT's information-gravity questions but are not equivalent to an SoCT memory field and do not establish PNT.

### 8. Gravitational Memory Effect / BMS Symmetry

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** General relativity predicts technically defined gravitational memory effects: after a burst of gravitational radiation, detector configurations can retain a lasting displacement or timing change. This effect is related to Bondi-Metzner-Sachs (BMS) asymptotic symmetries and soft-graviton relations.

**SoCT overlap.** Both use the concept of persistence or memory in relation to gravity.

**SoCT difference.** Gravitational-wave memory is a specific relativistic observable with defined asymptotic and waveform structure. SoCT uses "memory" more broadly as a proposed accumulated history of collapse-like events.

**Claim boundary.** Mainstream gravitational memory does not prove that gravity is memory, does not establish an SoCT memory field, and must not be presented as direct evidence for SoCT. It is a technical comparison point and a source of mathematical discipline for the word "memory."

### 9. Emergent Spacetime / Entanglement Geometry

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Work in holography and quantum information suggests that connected spacetime geometry may emerge from patterns of quantum entanglement. Van Raamsdonk's thought experiments, for example, connect decreasing entanglement with geometric separation and pinching-off in holographic settings.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT/PNT treats collapse, memory, entanglement residue, and spacetime structure as potentially connected.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT emphasizes event sequence and memory accumulation rather than reconstructing geometry from entanglement structure alone.

**Claim boundary.** Emergent-spacetime research motivates a comparison between information structure and geometry. It does not validate SoCT's proposed collapse-memory mechanism.

### 10. Entropic Gravity / Emergent Gravity

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Entropic/emergent-gravity proposals explore whether gravitational dynamics can emerge from information, entropy, holography, or underlying microscopic degrees of freedom rather than being fundamental in the standard sense.

**SoCT overlap.** Both ask whether familiar gravitational behavior could reflect deeper information-processing structure.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT does not currently define gravity as an entropic force. Its phenomenological lane instead asks whether effective gravitational behavior can correlate with accumulated collapse history through a memory variable.

**Claim boundary.** Entropic or emergent gravity does not prove SoCT, and agreement with a galaxy-scale anomaly would not by itself distinguish SoCT from other modified- or emergent-gravity models.

### 11. Dark Matter Alternatives / Modified Gravity

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Galaxy rotation curves, lensing, clusters, cosmology, and structure formation motivate particle dark matter as the standard framework while also supporting an active literature on modified-dynamics and modified-gravity alternatives such as MOND and relativistic extensions.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT's P1/P2 tracks examine whether age, radius, redshift, and environment can be organized by a memory-field-inspired effective-gravity model.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT is not MOND, not cold dark matter, and not yet a complete modified-gravity theory. Its proposed collapse-history interpretation must be formalized and confronted with the same multi-observable constraints applied to competing models.

**Claim boundary.** Dark-sector anomalies motivate SoCT tests but do not confirm SoCT. A successful fit to rotation curves alone would not establish a unique mechanism.

### 12. Cosmic Web / Void-Filament Structure

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Large-scale structure forms a cosmic web of voids, sheets/walls, filaments, and clusters through gravitational evolution from primordial density fluctuations in standard structure-formation models.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT/PNT uses void-filament-cluster environment as an observational scaffold for testing whether candidate memory-related quantities correlate with cosmic environment.

**SoCT difference.** Standard cosmology explains the web through gravitational instability, dark matter, expansion history, and initial conditions. SoCT adds a proposed memory-gradient interpretation that must generate an independent, quantitative signature.

**Claim boundary.** Cosmic-web structure does not prove SoCT. It supplies environment labels and comparison variables for tests such as DESI/Pantheon+ crossmatching.

### 13. Causal Set Theory / Planck Discreteness

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Causal set theory proposes that spacetime at the smallest scales may be represented by a locally finite causal order whose continuum approximation can recover Lorentzian geometry.

**SoCT overlap.** PNT uses Planck-scale information-packet and substrate language and explores discrete nucleation-like events.

**SoCT difference.** Causal set theory has a specific mathematical object and causal-order program. PNT adds speculative nucleation attempts, failed-event exhaust, and memory residue that are not implied by causal-set discreteness.

**Claim boundary.** Causal-set adjacency does not validate PNT. PNT must derive its own dynamics, conservation rules, continuum limit, and empirical predictions.

### 14. Loop Quantum Cosmology / Bounce Cosmology

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** In specific loop-quantum-cosmology models, quantum geometry replaces the classical big-bang singularity with a quantum bounce and yields deterministic evolution through the Planck regime.

**SoCT overlap.** P6 uses compression/bounce language when exploring parent-child universe transfer and black-hole interiors.

**SoCT difference.** Loop quantum cosmology has a formal Hamiltonian and quantum-geometry apparatus. SoCT/PNT cannot inherit "bounce" conclusions without deriving corresponding geometry, conservation conditions, junction behavior, and observable consequences.

**Claim boundary.** Bounce cosmology provides comparison material, not proof of black-hole child-universe transfer or PNT.

### 15. Integrated Information / Consciousness-Field Theories

**Mainstream / adjacent claim.** Integrated Information Theory (IIT) attempts to characterize consciousness using intrinsic causal/information structure and quantitative measures. Other consciousness models likewise seek operational bridges between physical organization and conscious state.

**SoCT overlap.** SoCT includes an observer-state or conscious-access channel that must become operationally measurable if it is to enter physics-facing experiments.

**SoCT difference.** SoCT does not reduce to IIT and should not adopt IIT's ontology or mathematical quantities without derivation. The current SoCT Hamiltonian treats observer-state dependence as a separate hypothesis channel rather than as established consciousness physics.

**Claim boundary.** IIT and other consciousness theories do not prove SoCT, a consciousness field, or consciousness-driven collapse. Their strongest role here is methodological: they show how consciousness hypotheses can be forced toward operational definitions.
